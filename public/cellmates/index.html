Statements

Tap a statement to pick that person, then tap their cell on the board. Statements cross themselves off once that person is placed.

    • Every row and every column holds exactly one person, the victim included.
    • Nobody can be on a hatched cell (table, lamp, piano, stove, trunk…).
    • "In a seat", "on a bench", "on a rug": on that object's own cell.
    • Beside an object: sharing an edge with it. Diagonally next to someone: touching corners.
    • Left of / above: anywhere further left or higher up.
    • The murderer is the only person alone with the victim in a room.

    Map key

      How to play

      • Each case is a floor plan of the train. Thick lines separate rooms.
      • Every row and every column holds exactly one person, victim included.
      • Nobody can be on a hatched cell: tables, lamps, pianos, stoves, trunks, crates, desks, washbasins, mail sacks, coal and the firebox fill their cell.
      • People can sit in seats, lie on bunks, stand on rugs and sit on benches, stools and suitcases. "Sitting on a bench" means on that object's own cell, never the cell next to it or below it.
      • Beside an object: in a cell that shares an edge with it.
      • Diagonally next to someone: the two cells touch at a corner.
      • Left of / above: in any column further left, or any row higher up, not necessarily next to each other.
      • Rows are numbered from the top, columns from the left.
      • The murderer is the only person who was alone with the victim in a room.

      Controls

      • Tap a person's statement to pick them, then tap their cell. Tap another cell later to move them. Their statement is crossed off while they are on the board.
      • × and the eraser under the board stay on until you tap them again.
      • Rows and columns that already have someone are shaded, because nobody else can go there.
      • Check tells you whether your placements fit. Accuse names the killer.
      • Keyboard: arrow keys move, a letter places that person, X marks, Backspace clears, Ctrl+Z undoes.
